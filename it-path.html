IT Support Learning Plan, 24 Weeks

This plan takes two learners with accounting degrees from beginner to ready to apply for entry-level IT help desk roles. It assumes 10 to 12 hours of study per week. Each week has one hands-on task. Each phase ends with a checkpoint. Learners should review each other's work weekly.

Check prices and dates yourself. Exam prices, course availability and program batch dates change often. Where a figure appears below, it is approximate and the official link is the source of truth.

The exam: CompTIA A+

A+ is the most commonly requested entry-level credential for help desk roles. It has two exams, Core 1 and Core 2. The current versions are 220-1201 and 220-1202, launched in March 2025.

ExamDomains and weights
Core 1 (220-1201)Mobile Devices 13%, Networking 23%, Hardware 25%, Virtualization and Cloud 11%, Hardware and Network Troubleshooting 28%
Core 2 (220-1202)Operating Systems 28%, Security 28%, Software Troubleshooting 23%, Operational Procedures 21%
  • Each exam has up to 90 questions in 90 minutes. A passing score is 675 out of 900.
  • List price is about 274 US dollars per exam, according to reseller and blog pages. Confirm on the official CompTIA page before buying.
  • Exams are delivered through Pearson VUE, at a test center or online from home. Check current Pakistan test centers at pearsonvue.com. Online testing needs a quiet room, a webcam and a stable connection.
  • Study with the free videos first. Buy an exam voucher only around week 18 to 20, when practice scores are consistently above 80 percent.

Programs in Pakistan

  • DigiSkills.pk: free courses from the Ignite program. Enrollment opens in batches that have filled within days, and the program was reported to be under review in January 2026. Register and watch for the next batch. Most courses target freelancing, so treat it as a supplement.
  • NAVTTC training portal: free courses through partner institutions. Deadlines vary. Look for IT support or computer hardware courses in your city.
  • Do not pay any employer or recruiter a fee. An offer that asks you to buy a kit to work from home is a scam.

Finding the first job

  • Search Rozee.pk, Indeed Pakistan and LinkedIn Jobs for IT Support Engineer, IT Assistant, IT Help Desk Officer and trainee titles. Many roles ask for about two years of experience, so apply to the assistant and trainee titles first.
  • Customer support or chat support work is a realistic first step. It builds the communication record that help desk employers ask for.
  • Credentials and a portfolio matter more than the degree for these roles. Keep the portfolio on GitHub: home-lab write-up, 5 or more runbooks, 10 sample tickets and knowledge-base articles, scripts, and the finance support playbook from week 22.
  • Upwork and Fiverr are a side channel only. PayPal is not available to Pakistan-based accounts, and new profiles with no reviews struggle. Fixed-price remote help desk work is rare.

Using the accounting background

Position yourselves as IT support for finance and accounting teams. Someone who understands a trial balance, a bank reconciliation and accounts payable can triage tickets that a general IT person cannot. Learn one accounting package well (Tally is common in Pakistan, Odoo Community is free and good for a home lab, QuickBooks has free training), plus Excel and basic SQL. After 12 to 18 months in support, the next steps include ERP application support, systems analyst and financial data analyst roles.

Budget

The minimum cash cost is the two A+ exam vouchers per person. Everything else in the plan can be free or audited. Do not buy ITIL certification, TryHackMe Premium or a DeepLearning.AI membership in the first 24 weeks unless a specific need comes up. ITIL can wait until you have a job, ideally with the employer paying.
