Selling on eBay from Pakistan

This guide covers how to open an account, get paid, list items, price them and ship them. Some rules for Pakistan-based sellers are not clearly published by eBay. Those are marked "Confirm" so you check them before spending money.

Open questions to confirm before you start.
  • Confirm that Pakistan appears in eBay's country list during registration.
  • One forum answer says an individual in Pakistan may need to be a registered business to export. This is a single unverified claim, and it conflicts with Payoneer offering individual accounts. Ask your bank or an accountant about State Bank of Pakistan, FBR (tax) and Customs rules, and about getting an NTN tax number if you sell regularly.
  • Confirm whether eBay International Shipping or the Global Shipping Program work for sellers shipping out of Pakistan. They list Pakistan as a destination. Treat them as unavailable until Seller Hub shows otherwise.

Step 1: Get paid, then register

  • eBay pays sellers through a bank account or Payoneer, depending on country. Payoneer has a Pakistan seller onboarding guide, which suggests Pakistani sellers are expected. See Payoneer for eBay and the eBay and Payoneer FAQ.
  • Payouts arrive in US dollars. You can then withdraw to a Pakistani bank. Payoneer says receiving eBay payouts is free. Check its fee for withdrawing to your bank.
  • PayPal is not the payout route for eBay.
  • Individuals usually need a CNIC, an address document less than 3 months old (bank statement or utility bill), and a Pakistani bank account. A company account needs an SECP certificate, an NTN certificate or business license, a recent address document, and a bank account in the exact business name with an IBAN starting "PK". See the Payoneer Pakistan company guide.
  • eBay has Personal and Business accounts. You can upgrade personal to business, but not the reverse. You also need a credit or debit card for fees. See eBay's cross-border selling guide.
  • Match the account type and name: an individual Payoneer account with a personal eBay account, a company account with a business eBay account.
  • Community reports say activation takes 2 to 4 weeks after you link Payoneer, and new accounts have selling limits that eBay emails to you. Treat both as approximate.
Do not sell through someone else's account. Using a friend's or relative's eBay account abroad, or opening extra accounts to get around limits, risks suspension, held funds, and tax and legal trouble for both people. eBay's multiple accounts policy bans registering accounts to avoid restrictions. If you want a US presence, a properly registered business needs legal and tax advice first.

Step 2: Understand the fees

The eBay US fee page shows these typical figures:

  • Final value fee: 13.6 percent in most categories for sales up to 7,500 dollars. Jewelry and Watches is 15 percent. Books, Movies and Music is 15.3 percent.
  • Per-order fee: 0.30 dollars for orders of 10 dollars or less, 0.40 dollars above that.
  • The first 250 listings each month are free, then 0.35 dollars each.

Fees differ by eBay site and seller type. Other sources quote slightly different rates, so check the fee view in your own Seller Hub. Always price an item as: what sold items go for, minus the fee, minus courier cost.

Step 3: Choose what to sell

  • Beginner guides suggest handicrafts, textiles, embroidered or block-printed fabric, leather goods, sports goods and jewelry. This is not demand data. Check demand yourself first (Step 4). Jewelry has the highest fee.
  • Avoid food and spices at the start. They are restricted and regulated on import.
  • Surgical instruments may count as medical devices and knives and blades are tightly regulated. Read the policy first.
  • Prohibited items include drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods and stolen property. Also avoid ivory, fur and wildlife products, cultural artifacts and fake brand-name goods. Read eBay's rules and restrictions. Breaking them can lead to removed listings or suspension.

Step 4: Research prices

  • On eBay, search for the item, open Advanced Search and tick "Sold items". Those are real selling prices.
  • Terapeak Product Research is free in Seller Hub and shows about 3 years of sold data. See the Terapeak announcement.

Step 5: Write good listings

  • Use a clear title with the words a buyer would search for.
  • Take several sharp photos from different angles on a plain background.
  • Pick the correct category and fill in every item specific (brand, size, color, material).
  • Describe condition honestly. State who pays import duties and taxes at the destination.
  • Upload tracking as soon as you ship, and reply to messages quickly.

Step 6: Ship and handle returns

  • Beginner guides name DHL, FedEx, TCS and Pakistan Post EMS. Get live quotes for your target countries and always use a tracked service. Do not trust claims about delivery times without a quote.
  • You must follow the laws of Pakistan and of the buyer's country. Export paperwork goes through Customs and your bank. Import duties and VAT vary, and the EU and UK are stricter.
  • Even if you say "no returns", eBay's Money Back Guarantee forces a refund when an item is not as described or arrives damaged. Return shipping from Pakistan is expensive, so photograph accurately and pack carefully.

A 30-day starter plan

Days 1 to 5. Decide on a personal or business account. Ask a bank or accountant about export proceeds and tax. Gather your CNIC, address document and IBAN. Research 3 product ideas with sold listings and Terapeak.
Days 6 to 10. Create the Payoneer account (company review takes about 3 business days). Register on eBay, link Payoneer with matching names, and add a card. Start the free eBay seller education modules while you wait for activation.
Days 11 to 20. Choose 5 to 10 items you can source and ship. Get courier quotes. Take photos and draft titles and descriptions. Set your return policy and plan packaging.
Days 21 to 30. Once activated, list your items at fixed prices within your limits. Ship fast with tracking. Review your first orders before listing more.

If eBay does not work out

  • Daraz is a domestic marketplace. Registration needs a CNIC or business NTN and bank details.
  • Etsy lists Pakistan for payments through Payoneer, but a community thread reports trouble applying. Check Etsy's current help pages.
  • Amazon opened to Pakistan-based merchants in 2021 (report). Rules may have changed.
Information checked October 2026. Rules and fees change, so confirm on the official pages linked above. This is general information, not legal or tax advice.
